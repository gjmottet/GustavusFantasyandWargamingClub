D&D at Gustavus

Our Dungeons & Dragons table runs on imagination, improv, and a healthy respect for the dice. We play 5th Edition with a heavy homebrew flavor — and because different members run different games, no two campaigns at our table look alike.

New to D&D? No problem. We'll walk you through character creation and have you rolling dice the same session.

  • Rotating Dungeon Masters — every member is welcome to run a game
  • Beginner-friendly — pre-generated characters available
  • Dice, books, and battle maps provided at the table
  • One-shots and short arcs between longer campaigns

🎲 Calling All Dungeon Masters

The club has no official Dungeon Master — and that's by design. Every member is invited to step behind the screen. Whether you want to run a one-shot, a short arc, or a full campaign, we'll give you a table, players, and a Sunday slot during our noon–8 PM library block. Pitch your idea at a session or get in touch.

📅 Featured Campaign — April & May 2027 · Six Parts

Geneva Mottet is offering a six-part campaign running through April and May 2027, kicking off alongside the club's launch. Sessions run during our Sunday noon–8 PM block at the library.

The Pitch: A sci-fi pocket dimension inspired by The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, where all of physics was figured out centuries ago by Isaac Newton — so the only rules that work are Newtonian mechanics and alchemy. Relativity, quantum mechanics, and anything dreamed up after 1727 simply don't function here. The campaign is set on a world called Alaska in an alternative future.

Newcomers are encouraged to join at the start of the arc. Watch this space for the session schedule and character creation night.
