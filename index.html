Warhammer 40K · Age of Sigmar

Warhammer at Gustavus

From pitched battles in the 41st Millennium to the Mortal Realms of Age of Sigmar, our Warhammer side covers casual pick-up games, narrative campaigns, and organized play. New players are always welcome — we'll teach you the rules and lend you an army to try.

All gaming takes place in the conference room of the Gustavus Public Library, which serves as both our painting station and our primary play space during our Sunday noon–8 PM block.

  • Casual and competitive games — 40K, Age of Sigmar, and skirmish formats
  • Painting and hobby nights with shared tables, lights, and terrain
  • Narrative campaigns and escalation leagues
  • Loaner armies available for newcomers

★ Warhammer Alliance Program · Ages 12–18

We're proud to run a Warhammer Alliance program for younger hobbyists aged 12 to 18. The Alliance is Games Workshop's official youth outreach program, and it gives younger players a structured, welcoming on-ramp into the hobby — building, painting, and playing alongside experienced members. Sessions are held in the conference room, which doubles as our painting and playing space during our Sunday library block.

Build

Learn to assemble, clean mold lines, and base your first models with tools and guidance on hand.

Paint

Step-by-step painting from primer to finished miniature. Brushes, paints, and palettes provided.

Play

Learn the core rules with small, fast games before scaling up to full battles.

D&D 5th Edition · Homebrew · Sci-Fi

D&D at Gustavus

Our Dungeons & Dragons table runs on imagination, improv, and a healthy respect for the dice. We play 5th Edition with a heavy homebrew flavor — and because different members run different games, no two campaigns at our table look alike.

New to D&D? No problem. We'll walk you through character creation and have you rolling dice the same session.

  • Rotating Dungeon Masters — every member is welcome to run a game
  • Beginner-friendly — pre-generated characters available
  • Dice, books, and battle maps provided at the table
  • One-shots and short arcs between longer campaigns

📅 Featured Campaign — April & May 2027 · Six Parts

Geneva Mottet is offering a six-part campaign running through April and May 2027, kicking off alongside the club's launch. Sessions run during our Sunday noon–8 PM block at the library.

The Pitch: A sci-fi pocket dimension inspired by The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, where all of physics was figured out centuries ago by Isaac Newton — so the only rules that work are Newtonian mechanics and alchemy. Relativity, quantum mechanics, and anything dreamed up after 1727 simply don't function here. The campaign is set on a world called Alaska in an alternative future.

Newcomers are encouraged to join at the start of the arc. Watch this space for the session schedule and character creation night.

Club Bylaws

The governing rules of Gustavus Fantasy and Wargaming. Click any article to expand it.

I Name and Purpose ⌄

Section 1: Name

The name of this organization shall be Gustavus Fantasy and Wargaming (hereinafter referred to as the "Club").

Section 2: Purpose

The purpose of the Club is to foster a welcoming, inclusive, and enthusiastic community for tabletop gaming. The Club focuses primarily on Dungeons & Dragons, Warhammer 40,000, Warhammer Age of Sigmar, and other tabletop wargames and roleplaying games. The Club aims to provide a dedicated space for casual play, competitive tournaments, campaign leagues, and hobby work such as miniature painting and terrain building.

II Membership ⌄

Section 1: Age Requirements

Membership is open to individuals aged 16 years and older. Members under the age of 18 must provide written parental or guardian consent to participate in Club events. The thematic elements, potential adult language, and potential financial impacts of wargaming hobbies are considered into the "Age Requirements" of the Club.

Section 2: Non-Discrimination

Membership shall not be denied to any individual on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, or disability.

Section 3: Member Responsibilities

All members agree to abide by the Club Code of Conduct, respect the venue, handle Club and other members' gaming properties with care, and contribute to a positive gaming environment.

Section 4: Dues (Optional)

The Executive Committee may establish annual or monthly membership dues to cover venue costs, terrain, prize support, or club upkeep. Dues must be approved by a majority vote of the membership. Dues are not enacted nor required at initial founding of the Club.

III Code of Conduct and Table Manners ⌄

Section 1: General Conduct

Members must treat others with respect. Harassment, bullying, cheating, or toxic behavior will not be tolerated. The golden rule of the Club is to "play nice, win nice, and lose nice."

Section 2: Venue Policies and Partner Expectations

  • Gustavus Public Library Compliance: The Club operates in alignment with the local community space. All members are strictly required to abide by all Gustavus Public Library policies, building regulations, and patron conduct expectations at all times.
  • Disruptive Behavior: Any behavior that violates the Library's rules, disrupts regular library operations, or compromises the Club's relationship with the library will result in immediate disciplinary action.

Section 3: Protection of Miniatures and Personal Property

  • Strict "No Touch" Policy: Members must never touch, move, alter, or paint another member's miniatures, models, dice, books, or personal gaming supplies without explicit, verbal permission from the owner.
  • Zero-Tolerance Vandalism: Any deliberate destruction, snapping, breaking, theft, or tampering of another person's miniatures or property is strictly prohibited.
  • Automatic Disciplinary Action: Violating this rule will result in immediate and permanent expulsion from the club venue and a total revocation of membership, with no warnings or probationary periods granted.

Section 4: Game Care and Upkeep

  • Food and drinks must be kept away from gaming surfaces, rulebooks, painted miniatures, and custom terrain, and must strictly follow the Library's food and beverage policies.
  • Members are responsible for cleaning up their play area, bagging components, and returning terrain or mats to their designated storage areas after use.
  • No game boxes or heavy luggage are to be left on the floor where they create tripping hazards.
IV Executive Committee (Officers) ⌄

Section 1: Officers

The Club shall be governed by an Executive Committee consisting of the following officers:

  • President (Grand Master): Oversees general operations, schedules meetings, acts as the primary contact for library staff, and ensures venue compliance.
  • Vice President (Warmaster): Assists the President, manages tournament/campaign organization, and oversees league rules.
  • Secretary (Lorekeeper): Handles communication, maintains membership records, and takes minutes during meetings.
  • Treasurer (Keeper of the Treasury): Manages club funds, tracks dues, and oversees expenditures for prize support or terrain.

Section 2: Term of Office

Officers shall be elected annually by a majority vote of the membership and will serve a term of one (1) year. Elections to be held at end of summer annually.

V Meetings and Gaming Sessions ⌄

Section 1: Regular Sessions

The Club will meet regularly at designated times and locations approved by the Executive Committee and scheduled in coordination with the Gustavus Public Library. Regular sessions are held Sundays from 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM, during hours when the library is normally closed to the public.

Section 2: Annual General Meeting

An Annual General Meeting (AGM) will be held once per year for officer elections, reviewing the club budget, and discussing changes to the bylaws. Elections to be held at end of summer annually.

VI Disciplinary Actions and Termination ⌄

Section 1: Suspension or Revocation

The Executive Committee reserves the right to suspend or revoke the membership of any individual who violates the Code of Conduct, causes intentional damage to club property, or disrupts the safety and enjoyment of the club.

Section 2: Immediate Removal Exception

In accordance with Article III, Section 3 (Property Damage) and Article III, Section 2 (Library Policy Violations), any member caught intentionally destroying miniatures or actively violating library conduct expectations will bypass the standard review process. They will face immediate removal from the premises by any officer and automatic, permanent termination of membership.

Section 3: Standard Process

For non-emergency violations, a unanimous vote by the Executive Committee is required to permanently expel a member. The individual in question has the right to present their case to the committee before a final vote is cast.

VII Amendments ⌄

Section 1: Bylaw Changes

These bylaws may be amended, altered, or repealed by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the members present at any official meeting, provided that notice of the proposed amendment has been given to the membership.

Everyone Deserves a Seat at the Table

Member Code of Conduct

All members are required to read, understand, and agree to this Code of Conduct. Our mission is to foster a welcoming, inclusive, and enthusiastic community for tabletop wargames and roleplaying games.

1 · General Conduct and Inclusivity

The Golden Rule: Members must always "play nice, win nice, and lose nice." Treat fellow players, opponents, and onlookers with the utmost respect.

Zero Tolerance for Toxicity: Harassment, bullying, cheating, discrimination, or toxic behavior of any kind will not be tolerated.

Non-Discrimination: The Club is open to all individuals regardless of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, or disability.

2 · Venue Policies and Library Compliance

Library Regulations: The Club operates out of the Gustavus Public Library. Members must strictly follow all library policies, building regulations, and patron conduct expectations at all times.

Disruption: Any behavior that breaks library rules or disrupts regular library operations is strictly forbidden and will result in immediate disciplinary action.

3 · Strict Protection of Personal Property

No-Touch Policy: You must never touch, move, alter, or paint another member's miniatures, models, dice, books, or gaming supplies without explicit, verbal permission from the owner.

Vandalism & Theft: Deliberate destruction, snapping, breaking, theft, or tampering with another person's miniatures or property is strictly prohibited.

Automatic Penalty: Violating this personal property rule will result in immediate, permanent expulsion from the venue and a total revocation of club membership without warnings or probation.

4 · Game Care, Safety, and Cleanliness

Food & Drink: Food and beverages must follow library rules and must be kept completely away from gaming surfaces, rulebooks, painted miniatures, and custom terrain.

Clean-Up: Members are responsible for cleaning their own play area. You must bag game components and return club terrain or mats to their designated storage areas after use.

Tripping Hazards: Keep the floor clear. No game boxes or heavy luggage should be left on the floor where they could cause someone to trip.

5 · Age Restrictions & Requirements

Ages 16+: Membership is open to individuals aged 16 and older.

Under 18 Notice: Members under 18 must provide written parental or guardian consent to participate. Parental discretion is advised regarding potential adult language, thematic elements, and the financial impacts of the wargaming hobby.

6 · Enforcement and Disciplinary Actions

The Executive Committee (Grand Master, Warmaster, Lorekeeper, Keeper of the Treasury) has the authority to enforce these rules.

Immediate Expulsion: Property damage, vandalism, or library policy violations will bypass standard reviews and result in immediate physical removal and permanent termination of membership.

Standard Process: For other violations, a unanimous vote by the Executive Committee can permanently expel a member after the individual has had a chance to present their case.
